Free Fire Texture Modding Guide

1 Locate Original File

Connect your Android device to a PC or use a file manager. Navigate to:
Android/data/com.dts.freefireth/files/ImageCache/ Find the skin or texture file you want to replace (usually ends in .astc or .bin).

2 Align & Compile

Upload that game file here. Upload your custom PNG/JPG skin. Click Auto-Resize & Align to match the exact dimensions, then click Build & Compile ASTC.

3 Replace & Play

Download the compiled ASTC file. Rename it to match the exact original filename, and copy it back to the same directory on your phone. Restart Free Fire to see your custom skin!
